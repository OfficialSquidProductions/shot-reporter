The next version keeps project information, take logs, camera settings, continuity notes, report checks, and exports together.

The downloads above are previous alpha builds. This report-only update has not yet been published. The Windows x64 download remains an unsigned manual MSI; Mac is signed and notarized.

iPhone, iPad and Mac TestFlight remain available to the invited account owner.

The record behind the shot.

  • Log scene and take labels, camera rolls, lens and exposure settings, and MOS/pickup/tail-slate flags.
  • Keep camera remarks, DP selections, continuity notes, and editorial notes separately.
  • Use Quick, Standard, and Full take views without losing recorded information.
  • Complete a student camera report with a missing-field checklist and self-review.
  • Preview a landscape PDF, export CSV/HTML, and transfer editable .camreport files deliberately.
  • Open The Last Light demo: the eight-scene script and 16-shot plan shared with Production Desk and Scenic, plus three completed 3A takes with take 2 circled.

Projects stay on your device. No account, subscription, cloud sync, or telemetry. Keep an independent local backup.

Platform status

  • iPhone / iPad: native field alpha · iOS / iPadOS 17+ · owner TestFlight available.
  • Mac: reporting alpha 0.1.1 · macOS 14+ · universal Intel / Apple silicon · signed, notarized download.
  • Android phones / tablets: field alpha · Android 8+ · signed APK download · tested on Android 15 phone/tablet emulators.
  • Windows: desktop alpha · Windows 10+ x64 · bundled runtime · unsigned manual MSI download.

Physical device and camera testing is still needed. Desktop validation uses isolated report storage; keep independent report backups. Automatic update delivery remains subject to the platform signing and native upgrade gates described in the release notes.
