Shot Reporter 0.2.0-alpha.10 improves trash management, readable PDF reports and complete project handoff.

- Settings → Clear Trash asks for confirmation before permanently removing deleted reports and their previous local snapshots. Saved reports stay intact. Exports, legacy recovery files and external backups are kept.
- Deleted reports expire after 14 days, checked when the app opens or becomes active and hourly while open. Existing trash gets a full fresh 14 days on first opening this update. Restoring cancels cleanup; deleting again starts a new timer.
- Landscape PDFs have a production overview and labeled per-take camera/media/lens, recording-settings and notes sections. Long values and notes wrap onto labeled continuation pages.
- .camreport exports include the entire selected project/report model, including hidden Movie/TV values, camera defaults, all take settings/notes, flags, review state, history and preserved naming data. Import → Open as separate editable report is the complete handoff path. Media, scripts/attachments, other saved reports, local trash, device preferences and previous local recovery snapshots are outside the format. Conflicting edits must be opened separately rather than merged.

Apple marketing version 0.2.0, build 13. Local SQLite schema advances to 3; portable .camreport remains schema 1 with a 32 MiB limit. Older app builds cannot open the migrated local database. Mac direct builds use the authenticated signed Sparkle channel; TestFlight builds use TestFlight. Native QA uses synthetic reports and never purges owner trash.
